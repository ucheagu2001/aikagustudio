November 2026 — Current Studio Focus

Exploring E-Waste: Building 3D Works and Mixed-Media Reliefs

For the foreseeable future, my primary studio focus is centered heavily on exploring e-waste—transforming discarded motherboards, circuit boards, computer keyboards, phone components, and tangled wiring into powerful three-dimensional works and mixed-media bas-reliefs.

Transforming Industrial Scraps into Totems of Heritage

Biography & Artistic Practice

Aik Agu — Studio Practice

Aik Agu is a Nigerian-Canadian visual artist whose multidisciplinary practice explores African identity, cultural memory, spirituality, and symbolism.

His work spans painting, sculpture, wood-panel art, mixed media, and upcycled materials, transforming discarded and everyday objects into meaningful works of art.

Heritage & Visual Language

Deeply influenced by his Nigerian heritage, Agu incorporates elements of Nsibidi and Uli into contemporary compositions, using traditional visual languages to explore ancestry, identity, and spirituality.

Material Transformation

With formal training in Art and Design, specializing in Sculpture, Agu approaches his practice as both an artist and a cultural storyteller, giving forgotten materials new life.
